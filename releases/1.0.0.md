First release of Digital Downloads for Swell.

- **Files and links on any product.** Add links to any https address, or upload files into your own Amazon S3, Cloudflare R2, Backblaze B2 or Wasabi bucket. Give a file to every variant or to one.
- **Delivered after payment.** Paid and free orders get a "Downloads ready" email and a private downloads page. Opening the page never uses up a download; only the Download and Open buttons count.
- **Private downloads.** Every download is checked against the order and its limits first. Uploaded files get a link that expires after 5 minutes.
- **Download limits and access periods,** set for the store and overridden per product.
- **License keys.** Generate a key per purchase or sell imported keys, with activation limits, expiry, a low-stock warning, and orders stopped when imported keys run out. A license API validates, activates and deactivates keys.
- **Subscriptions.** Access follows the subscription, and subscription keys are extended at each renewal.
- **Updates.** Replacing a file or editing a link reaches past buyers at once, and Email past buyers about an update tells them.
- **Order tools.** Resend the downloads email, reset counts, extend, revoke and restore access. Cancellations and full refunds remove access automatically. Download access and a download log are listed under Orders.

**Getting started:** set your download limits under Apps → Digital Downloads → Settings, then add files or links on a product's Digital delivery tab. To upload files, connect a bucket under File storage first.
